04. Featured Projects

Featured Project · Deployed at Hospital del Mar

SlicerSEEG

Problem: Neurosurgeons spend 4+ hours per patient manually identifying SEEG electrode positions in post-operative CT scans, creating critical bottlenecks in epilepsy surgery workflows.

Approach: Built a 3D Slicer extension combining a MONAI-based 3D U-Net for brain extraction (93.6% Dice), Random Forest threshold prediction (R² = 0.99), LightGBM confidence scoring (98.8% within 2mm), and DBSCAN + Louvain clustering for trajectory reconstruction.

Impact: Reduces electrode identification from 4+ hours to ~30 minutes. Now in clinical use at Hospital del Mar's Epilepsy Unit in collaboration with the Center for Brain and Cognition (UPF). Bachelor's thesis graded 10/10 with honors.

  • Python
  • 3D Slicer
  • MONAI
  • LightGBM

Featured Project · Validated at Hospital del Mar

Brain Mask 3D Segmentation

Problem: Post-surgical brain imaging contains artifacts and metallic electrode interference that make accurate brain extraction extremely difficult for standard tools.

Approach: Trained a 3D U-Net using MONAI and PyTorch with leave-one-patient-out validation. Handles CT artifacts and metallic electrode interference through specialized augmentation strategies.

Impact: Achieves 93.6% Dice accuracy, reducing processing time from 4+ hours to under 1 minute. Foundational component supporting neurosurgeons in drug-resistant epilepsy treatment planning. Deployed with a clinical-grade web interface.

  • PyTorch
  • MONAI
  • 3D U-Net
  • Computer Vision
Volumetric Brain Segmentation

Featured Project

Synthetic Patient Cohort Generation

Problem: Rare diseases suffer from extremely limited patient datasets, making it nearly impossible to train robust ML models while respecting patient privacy.

Approach: Implemented and compared GANs, VAEs, and diffusion models for generating realistic, privacy-preserving synthetic patient cohorts. Validated synthetic data quality against real clinical distributions.

Impact: Enables researchers to train models on statistically representative synthetic data for rare diseases where real patient data is scarce or restricted.
